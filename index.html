CORE5: Reimagining Healthcare Through Biomedical Innovation.

CORE5 is a team of five biomedical design students exploring unmet healthcare needs, researching challenges and developing innovative approaches to improve healthcare, with a focus on Uganda and the wider African healthcare environment.

This site documents our Biodesign process: The Problem, Needs Assessment, Our Innovation, Design Process, Research, Our Team and Contact.
